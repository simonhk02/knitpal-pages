BrewDial Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

BrewDial collects no personal data. Your bean library, brew logs, taste notes, and charts live entirely on your device. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

What is stored and where

  • Bean profiles — names, roasters, origins, roast dates, and freshness notes are stored locally on your device.
  • Brew records — dose, grind setting, time, yield, method, and taste ratings are stored locally on your device.
  • Everything else — settings and preferences never leave your device either. We operate no servers that receive your data. If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it.

Permissions

BrewDial requests no special iOS permissions. The app works entirely without access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Purchases

BrewDial Pro subscriptions (US$9.99/year) and the lifetime unlock (US$29.99) are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Analytics and tracking

None. BrewDial contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising identifiers, no crash-reporting SDKs that send data to third parties, and it does not track you across apps or websites. There is no account system — you never sign in.

Children

BrewDial does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date. Since the app collects no data, future changes will never retroactively affect data already on your device.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@brewdial.app.
