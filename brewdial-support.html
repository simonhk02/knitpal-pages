Frequently asked questions

What does BrewDial Pro unlock?

The free version includes two beans with full logging — the dial-in guide, freshness tracking, and trend charts are all included, no feature is locked. BrewDial Pro removes the bean limit so you can keep your whole rotation in the app:

  • Pro Annual — US$9.99/year
  • Pro Lifetime — US$29.99 one-time purchase, never renews

To upgrade: open BrewDial, try to add a third bean (or tap any Pro badge), and choose a plan on the paywall.

How does the dial-in guide work?
  1. Open your bean and tap Log Brew (espresso, V60 pour-over, AeroPress, French press, or moka pot).
  2. Enter your recipe — dose, grind setting, time, yield — then tag the taste: sour / under-extracted, bitter / over-extracted, weak / thin, or balanced.
  3. BrewDial shows a specific adjustment for your method — e.g. grind finer, or keep the grind and extend the yield — so your next brew fixes the problem instead of guessing at it.

Suggestions get sharper as you log more brews for the same bean, and the trend charts show your dial-in converging over time.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top → Subscriptions.
  3. Select BrewDial → Cancel Subscription.

You keep Pro access until the end of the period you already paid for. The free tier (two beans, full logging) remains available afterwards, and all your data stays on your device. The Lifetime purchase is one-time and never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
  1. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
  2. Open BrewDial, go to the paywall (it appears when you add a third bean), and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom.

Both the yearly subscription and the Lifetime purchase restore this way. If restore fails, check your connection and try again — then contact us if it still doesn't unlock.

Is my data backed up?

All BrewDial data is stored locally on your device and is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer backup). See the Privacy Policy for details — BrewDial has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

Does BrewDial work offline?

Yes. Logging, the dial-in guide, freshness tracking, and charts all run on-device. Internet is only needed to purchase Pro or restore a purchase.
