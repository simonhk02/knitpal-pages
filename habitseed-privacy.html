HabitSeed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The short version: your data never leaves your device. HabitSeed has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. We cannot see your habits because we never receive them.

1. Data We Collect

None. HabitSeed does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal data on our servers — because we don't operate any servers.

2. Where Your Data Lives

All of your habits, check-ins, streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it (subject to any device-level backups you manage yourself through iOS).

3. Analytics and Tracking

HabitSeed contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting third-party services, no advertising frameworks, and no tracking of any kind. We do not use cookies, device identifiers, or fingerprinting.

4. Accounts

HabitSeed does not require or support user accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to leak.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, iOS schedules them locally on your device. Habit names and reminder times are never sent anywhere.

6. CSV Export

CSV export is entirely user-initiated. When you export a habit's history, the file is created on your device and shared only through the destinations you choose via the iOS share sheet. We never see the contents.

7. Purchases

HabitSeed Pro purchases (yearly subscription or lifetime unlock) are processed by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, payment details, or any personal information — only an anonymous purchase state from Apple's StoreKit.

8. Third-Party Services

HabitSeed integrates no third-party services that collect data. The only external system involved is Apple's App Store / StoreKit for purchases.

9. Children's Privacy

Because HabitSeed collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. The app is suitable for users of all ages.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Given the app's design, meaningful changes are unlikely — there is simply nothing to change.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@habitseed.app.
