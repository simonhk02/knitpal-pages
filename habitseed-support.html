Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my first habit?

Tap the + button on the home screen, type a name (e.g. "Read 10 minutes"), pick a reminder time if you want one, and tap Save. That's it — no setup wizard, no account.

How do I check off a habit?

Tap the habit's circle on the home screen. One tap and it's done for today. Tap again to undo if it was an accident.

How many habits can I track for free?

The free version tracks up to 3 habits forever — no trial period, no feature locks. HabitSeed Pro removes the limit.

What does HabitSeed Pro cost?

Pro unlocks unlimited habits for US$9.99/year, or US$24.99 once for lifetime access. Both are purchased through the App Store and work on all your devices via the same Apple ID.

I bought Pro on my iPhone — how do I get it on my iPad?

Install HabitSeed on the second device with the same Apple ID, open the app, go to the Pro screen, and tap Restore Purchases. No account needed.

How does the streak flame work?

Check in every day and your streak grows by one. Miss a day and the streak resets to zero — but your history on the 12-week grid stays, so you can always see how far you've come.

What is the 12-week grid?

Each habit has a grid showing the last 12 weeks, one square per day. Green squares are days you showed up. It's the fastest way to see your consistency at a glance.

My reminders aren't firing. How do I fix this?

Open iOS Settings → Notifications → HabitSeed and make sure notifications are allowed. Then check the reminder time inside the habit's settings in the app. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device — they work offline and never leave your phone.

How do I export my data?

Open a habit, tap Export CSV, and choose where to send the file. The CSV contains every check-in date for that habit.

Where is my data stored? Is there a backup?

All data lives on your device and is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer). HabitSeed itself sends nothing to any server. Deleting the app deletes its data, so export a CSV first if you want a copy.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your device. Since nothing is stored on our servers (we don't have any), that's a complete deletion.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → HabitSeed and tap Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. The lifetime option never renews, so there's nothing to cancel.
