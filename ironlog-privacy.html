IronLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The short version: your training data never leaves your device. We can't see it, because we never receive it.

1. Data Stays On Your Device

All workout data you enter into IronLog — exercises, sets, reps, weights, templates, personal records, and history — is stored locally on your iPhone. IronLog does not operate any server that receives, stores, or processes your training data.

2. No Analytics, No Tracking, No Ads

IronLog contains:

  • No third-party analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar)
  • No advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • No crash-reporting services that transmit device data
  • No tracking of any kind, as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework

3. No Account

IronLog does not require or offer an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier.

4. Purchases

IronLog Pro subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store (StoreKit). We never see, collect, or store your payment information. Apple may provide us with anonymized, aggregated sales figures, which contain no personal data.

5. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, deleting the IronLog app deletes all of your data. There is nothing to delete on our side — we hold nothing.

6. Children's Privacy

IronLog does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, no parental consent mechanism is required.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@ironlog.app.
