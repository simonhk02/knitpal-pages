Getting Started

How do I log a workout?

Create a template (or start an empty workout), tap an exercise, enter weight and reps, and hit Done. The rest timer starts automatically after each set.

How do templates work?

A template is a saved workout — exercises, sets, and target reps. Build it once, then start that workout in two taps. Free includes 1 template; Pro unlocks unlimited templates.

Is my data synced or backed up?

All data is stored on your device. It is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer). Deleting the app deletes your data, so keep device backups on.

Features

How does PR detection work?

IronLog compares every set against your full history for that exercise — heaviest weight, most reps at a weight, and best estimated 1RM. When you beat one, a PR badge appears instantly. No setup needed.

How is my estimated 1RM calculated?

IronLog uses the Epley formula (weight × (1 + reps/30)) on your best recent sets, then charts the trend over time so you can see strength gains even between max attempts.

Can I adjust the rest timer?

Yes. Set a default rest duration and override it per exercise. The timer auto-starts when you complete a set and notifies you when it's time to lift again.

What does Volume Analytics show?

Total tonnage (sets × reps × weight) per session and per exercise, over time. Use it to confirm your workload is actually increasing — the core of progressive overload.

Pro & Billing

What do I get with Pro?

Unlimited templates, complete workout history (free keeps 30 days), and full analytics. US$14.99/year or US$39.99 once for lifetime — same features either way.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open IronLog → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro. Lifetime unlocks restore the same way.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → IronLog → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Lifetime purchases never expire and never need cancelling.

I bought Pro but the app still shows Free. What do I do?

Tap Settings → Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with your Apple receipt and we'll sort it out.
