KnitPal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

KnitPal collects no personal data. Your projects, rows, and patterns live on your device and in your private iCloud account. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

What is stored and where

  • Projects and counters — stored on your device and synced via your private iCloud container (Apple CloudKit). We never see this data.
  • Imported pattern photos — processed on-device for text/chart recognition and stored only in your local library / iCloud sync.

Permissions

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — used only for the optional voice row-counting feature. Speech is recognized on-device; audio is never recorded, stored, or sent to any server.
  • Camera / Photo Library — used only when you choose to import a pattern from a photo. Photos are processed on-device.

Purchases

KnitPal Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Analytics and tracking

None. KnitPal contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising identifiers, no crash-reporting SDKs that send data to third parties, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

KnitPal does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date.
