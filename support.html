Frequently asked questions

How do I use voice row counting?

Voice counting is a KnitPal Pro feature:

  1. Open your project and tap the microphone icon (or enable Voice Counting in project settings).
  2. The first time, iOS asks for Microphone and Speech Recognition permission — tap Allow. Recognition runs entirely on your device; nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  3. While you knit or crochet, say "count" to add a row, "undo" to go back one, and "stop" to turn the microphone off.

Tip: voice counting works best in a reasonably quiet room, with the device within arm's reach.

How do I import a pattern from a photo?

In any project (Pro), tap Import Pattern → Take Photo or Choose from Library. KnitPal reads the text/chart on-device and attaches it to your project. For best results, photograph the pattern flat, in good light, filling the frame.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
  1. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
  2. Open KnitPal, go to the paywall (it appears when you tap any Pro feature), and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom.

Both the yearly subscription and the Lifetime purchase restore this way. If restore fails, check your connection and try again — then contact us if it still doesn't unlock.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top → Subscriptions.
  3. Select KnitPal Pro → Cancel Subscription.

You keep Pro access until the end of the period you already paid for. The free tier (one project) remains available afterwards. The Lifetime purchase is one-time and never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Does the free trial convert automatically?

Yes — like all App Store trials, the 7-day free trial converts to the paid yearly subscription unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). You will not be charged during the trial itself.

Is my data private?

Yes. KnitPal has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything is stored on your device and in your private iCloud. See the Privacy Policy.
