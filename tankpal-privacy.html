TankPal Privacy Policy

Effective date:

TankPal ("the app") is an aquarium logging app for iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles — which is very little, by design.

1. Data Storage: On-Device Only

All data you enter into TankPal — tank profiles, water parameter readings (pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, temperature, GH, KH), water change history, livestock and plant records, notes and photos — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that receive or store your aquarium data. If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it (unless you have exported it yourself).

2. No Analytics, No Tracking, No Ads

TankPal contains:

  • No analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar)
  • No advertising networks or ad identifiers
  • No third-party tracking of any kind
  • No crash-reporting services that transmit data off your device beyond Apple's own opt-in system diagnostics

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or usage behavior. There is no account system — you never sign in.

3. Purchases

TankPal Pro subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store and StoreKit. We never see, collect, or store your payment information. Apple may provide us with anonymized, aggregated sales figures, which cannot identify you.

4. Data Export

You can export your data (CSV) at any time from within the app. Exported files are saved where you choose on your device or iCloud Drive and are under your control.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional) — used solely for water change and maintenance reminders you configure. Scheduled and delivered locally on-device.
  • Photos / Camera (optional) — used only when you attach photos to livestock or tank records. Photos remain on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

TankPal does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because the app collects no personal information at all.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Since the app collects no data, future changes will never retroactively affect data already on your device.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@tankpal.app
