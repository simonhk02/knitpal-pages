FAQ

How do I add a second tank?

Multiple tanks are a TankPal Pro feature. The free version includes one tank with full logging. To add more tanks:

  1. Open Settings in TankPal.
  2. Tap Upgrade to Pro.
  3. Choose Pro Annual (US$12.99/year) or Pro Lifetime (US$34.99 one-time).

Once Pro is active, tap the tank switcher at the top of the home screen and choose Add Tank. Pro includes unlimited tanks and unlimited livestock entries.

How do I export my data to CSV?
  1. Open the tank you want to export.
  2. Go to Settings > Export Data (or the export icon on the tank screen).
  3. Choose what to include: water parameters, water change history, and/or livestock records.
  4. Tap Export CSV. The file is generated on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  5. Save it to Files/iCloud Drive or share it via the standard iOS share sheet.

Exports work offline and are available in both the free and Pro versions.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. To cancel:

  1. Open your iPhone's Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select TankPal and tap Cancel Subscription.

You keep Pro access until the end of the current billing period. The free version (one tank, full logging) remains available afterwards, and all your data stays on your device. Note: the Pro Lifetime option is a one-time purchase and never needs cancelling.

Is my data backed up?

All TankPal data is stored locally on your device and is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer backup). We recommend exporting a CSV periodically if your records are important to you.

Does TankPal work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on-device — logging, charts, reminders, and exports. Internet is only needed to purchase Pro or restore purchases.

I bought a new phone. How do I restore Pro?

Open TankPal, go to Settings > Restore Purchases, and sign in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
